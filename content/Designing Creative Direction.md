Capturing creative direction may be one of the most important parts of developing your creative strategy for a client as your creative direction is what will impact your work most as a designer.  Creative Direction is the portion of client onboarding that most directly translates into your work—the typography, the layouts, the visuals—they compose 90% of your work.

In order to learn how to design creative direction well, *spend 15 minutes creating the visual direction, 15 minutes creating the layout direction, and 15 minutes creating the typography direction for sites you know well—sites that you can visualize the aesthetic of.*   To make this exercise effective, you must 1) understand the website’s aesthetic well.  If you have not paid close attention to a particular website/do not have one in mind for this exercise, it will likely be too hard to do on any normal website.

Revisit the website for 5 minutes and ==pay close attention== to the design—the typography, the imagery, the layout.  Then, close the website and go onto pinterest and develop creative directions for each.  Your goal is to end up with direction presentations that could take a client from nothing to the site aesthetic you matter.

The exercise will teach you to **deeply understand** aesthetics and the minor details that set each of them apart.
