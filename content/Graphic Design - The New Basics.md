This book was written to explore the universal elements of design and techniques graphic designers can use to dig deeper beyond superficial solutions to common problems.

# Formstorming 
>Formstorming moves the maker through automatic, easily conceived notions, toward recognizable yet nuanced concepts, to surprising results that compel us with their originality.

**Formstorming** is a visual thinking technique that teases out recognizable yet deeper, more nuanced concepts through iteration and transformation.

## Hundred Iterations
Come up with a hundred different iterations of an idea.

You can see the author goes from the obvious (the word, yolk, physical egg) and starts gradually moving towards more nuanced concepts that are related yet infinitely more interesting (hard-boiled, egg-benedict, the texture of an egg carton, humpty dumpty, goose and the egg, egg chair, whisk, pan).

This iteration technique forces the designer to walk through the surface level and into the territory of the more interesting.

## Dailies
This is a graphic designer’s version of a **daily practice**.  Designers are encouraged to define the framework, medium, and format for a daily practice and continue it for at least two weeks.  

![[a-record-a-day.png]]
*A designer challenged himself to match the tenor and moxy of a collection of his favorite albums using color, composition, and custom typography on a series of daily LP designs.*

## Process Verbs
After developing a cohesive visual design, challenge yourself to create 12 iterations of it using a list of action verbs (like tear, rotate, cut, copy, reflect, multiply, compress, disperse, warp).  This can reveal more interesting pathways

## Alterego
An alter ego is an idea that **undermines**, **amplifies**, or **rediscovers** a part of the design/idea. 

> Getting stuck on superficial ideas is a universal problem for designers.  These are techniques that are developed to force you past those barriers and roadblocks in a fun, yet exciting way.
