[[The Brand Gap]] by Marty Neumeier is the book that introduced me to brand strategy and how to think about branding from a strategic point of view.

> Different, I Like It

I’ve realized that a large portion of what makes good design is **adding differences**.  A webpage with uniform spacing, font, text-size isn’t very interesting or cognitively stimulating.  As humans, our brain has evolved to **pattern match** on autopilot.  We are constantly grouping stimuli together in order to lessen the noise.

This is also why **we tend to like things that are different**.  Something different stands out from the crowd and grabs our attention.

> The Evolution of Design

Design used to have four main goals in mind: to identify, to inform, to entertain, and to persuade.   Neumeier says branding adds a fifth: **to differentiate**.
