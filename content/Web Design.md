Finding art in the process of [[Web design]] took me a bit.

At first, web development was filled with spiritless wrangling of React and state management, which didn’t pose much of a challenge.

But then I fell into [[Design]] and started pushing the limits of what I knew of the web.  That’s where I began to realize, true [[Web Design]] was hard.

You can create noise with SVGs, give it a light source, and change how it looks accordingly.  It’s almost like an entire 3D environment in the palm of your SVG tag.

Creating web native [elements wasn’t easy either](https://css-tricks.com/different-ways-to-get-css-gradient-shadows/).
